Organization sharing turns an individual workspace into a shared one. Multiple people work from the same content library, lead lists, brand assets, and integrations.

To find it, open [app.agentweb.pro](https://app.agentweb.pro), click the gear icon in the bottom-left sidebar to open **Settings**, then select the **Organization** tab. You'll see the member list, role controls, and the invite button.

## Roles

Every person in an organization holds one of three roles: **owner**, **admin**, or **member**. An organization can have any number of owners and any number of admins.

| Capability | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Use the platform for their own work | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| See who is in the organization | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| See content the organization shares | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Invite and remove members | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Promote someone to admin, or demote them | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Approve or reject domain-join requests | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Change organization settings and sharing rules | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| See and manage every member's connected accounts | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Edit or delete another member's items | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| **Grant or revoke the owner role** | ✓ | — | — |
| **Remove an owner** | ✓ | — | — |
| **Delete the organization** | ✓ | — | — |

### Owner vs admin

Admins run everything day to day. The three rows in bold are the whole difference.

An admin cannot make anyone an owner, including themselves. That restriction is deliberate: owners can delete the organization and admins cannot, so allowing an admin to mint an owner would hand them that power by a side door.

### What members can and cannot do

Members are not restricted from using AgentWeb. They can run tasks, schedules, the marketing hub, social posts, SEO and CRM for their own work, and they can see whatever the organization has chosen to share.

What members cannot do is administer the organization. Inviting and removing people, changing settings, and changing anyone's role are reserved to owners and admins.

Members also see a narrower view of connected accounts. A member sees only the accounts explicitly shared with the organization. Owners and admins see every member's connected accounts, so they can decide what becomes a shared resource without passing credentials around.

<Note>
  If someone needs broader access, there are three different things to ask for, and they are not the same: **editor** on one specific area, **admin** for organization management, or **owner** for the actions in bold above.
</Note>

## Editors are a separate grant, not a role

"Editor" is not a fourth role. It is a per-area permission you grant to a named person on top of whatever role they already hold.

Each shareable area has its own editor list, so you can let a member edit the knowledge base without giving them any organization-management power at all.

| Area | Shared with the org by default | Can be turned off |
| --- | :-: | :-: |
| Knowledge base | ✓ | ✓ |
| Templates | ✓ | ✓ |
| Landing pages | ✓ | ✓ |
| Work plans | ✓ | ✓ |
| Skills | ✓ | ✓ |
| Analytics | ✓ | ✓ |
| Brand guidelines | ✓ | Always on |
| Market strategy | ✓ | Always on |

Brand guidelines and market strategy are read-only for members unless an owner or admin adds them as an editor for that area. The same is true of the knowledge base, templates and landing pages.

## Every organization keeps at least one owner

An organization must always have an owner. The last owner cannot be demoted, removed, or leave until another owner is promoted first. Any of those attempts is refused with a message telling you to promote someone else.

There is no minimum number of admins. An organization can have zero.

## Billing

One member anchors the organization's subscription. Their row carries a **Billing** badge next to their role.

Everyone else in the organization runs on that person's plan, and everyone's usage pools onto that plan's allowance. This is true for admins and second owners exactly as it is for members. Nobody else needs their own subscription.

Two consequences worth knowing:

- The billing owner's role cannot be changed. Their row shows a badge instead of a role dropdown, because every change the server would accept there does nothing.
- There is no "transfer ownership" button. To hand the organization over, promote someone else to owner, then leave or be removed. Billing moves to a remaining owner automatically at that point.

<Warning>
  If the billing owner is on a paid plan, they cannot leave or be removed while that subscription is live. A card cannot be handed to someone else. Have another owner start their own subscription first, then the original billing owner can leave. If you're stuck, email [support@agentweb.pro](mailto:support@agentweb.pro) and we can switch it over.
</Warning>

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings, then Organization">
    Click the gear icon in the bottom-left sidebar, then the **Organization** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their work email and send the invite">
    The invite takes an email address only. You do not pick a role here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    The invitee gets an email from `no-reply@agentweb.pro` with a signup link. Once they sign in, they have access to the shared workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set their role">
    Everyone joins as a **member**. To make someone an admin or an owner, change their role on their row after they accept.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Domain-join requests work the same way. When someone from your company's email domain asks to join and an owner or admin approves them, they join as a member.

## Change a member's role

Find the person in the member list and pick a new role from the dropdown on their row. The change takes effect immediately.

What you see on that dropdown depends on who you are:

- **Owners** see Owner, Admin and Member.
- **Admins** see Admin and Member. The Owner option is not offered, and an admin gets no dropdown at all on an owner's row.
- **Members** get no role controls.
- **Nobody** can change their own role, and nobody gets a dropdown on the billing owner's row.

Promoting someone to owner asks for confirmation first, because owners can delete the organization.

## Remove a member

Click the trash icon on their row. Before the removal goes through, you decide what happens to the items they own, one area at a time:

- **Transfer** them to you
- **Delete** them
- **Leave** them on the person's own account

The counts are shown per area (knowledge base, templates, landing pages, work plans, skills, funnels) so you can see what is at stake before confirming.

Removing someone does not delete their personal AgentWeb account. They lose access to your organization, and keep anything you chose to leave with them.

Only an owner can remove an owner. An admin who tries is refused.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can we have two admins?">
    Yes. An organization can have any number of admins and any number of owners.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Someone needs to see what's going on but shouldn't change anything">
    There is no read-only oversight role today. Members can see shared content and the member list, but not other members' connected accounts. If someone needs full visibility across the organization, make them an admin.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I hand my organization over to someone else?">
    Promote them to owner, then leave. There is no separate transfer step. If you're the billing owner on a paid plan, see the billing warning above first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="An invite never arrived">
    Ask the invitee to check spam. Invites come from `no-reply@agentweb.pro`. You can cancel and re-send a pending invite from **Settings**, then **Organization**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are my personal drafts visible to the team?">
    Only content in a shared area is visible to other members, and each shared area can be turned off for the whole organization. Brand guidelines and market strategy are always shared.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I belong to more than one organization?">
    No. One AgentWeb account belongs to one organization at a time. Email [support@agentweb.pro](mailto:support@agentweb.pro) if you need this.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
