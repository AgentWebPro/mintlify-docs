This playbook is for B2B founders who are pre-product or pre-scale — from the moment you decide to build, through closing your first ten paying customers. It is not traditional outbound. There is no cold email drip, no BDR team, no CRM you need to learn. The approach is: establish your credibility on LinkedIn, build a network of ICPs, enrich and manage those leads, and prepare for every conversation.

Emma handles the execution at each stage. You bring the founder context — your story, your ICP intuition, your willingness to have real conversations.

<Tip>
  **This is a sell-before-build approach.** You don't need a finished product to start. You need a founder profile that signals credibility, a growing network of ICPs, and conversations that teach you what to build.
</Tip>

## Before you start

Complete these one-time setup steps. Every stage of the playbook depends on them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete onboarding">
    Run through [brand onboarding](/onboarding/brand-onboarding) so Emma has your brand guidelines, ICP personas, and market strategy. The richer your ICPs — job titles, industries, company size, buying triggers — the better every downstream step works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your founder profile">
    Make sure your founder name and LinkedIn profile URL are in **Brand Hub → Brand Guidelines → Founder profile**. Emma reads this at the start of several work plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect LinkedIn Outreach">
    Go to **Settings → Connected Accounts** and connect **LinkedIn Outreach** (this is the Unipile connection, separate from LinkedIn posting). Stages 1, 2, and 4 all need it — the profile audit reads your live LinkedIn profile through it, outreach sends connection requests through it, and the decode playbook gets richer profile data with it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: connect Fathom">
    If you use [Fathom](https://fathom.video) for meeting recordings, connect it in **Settings → Connected Accounts**. Stage 4's decode playbook checks your Fathom history for prior conversations with the person you're about to meet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: connect Gmail or Outlook">
    Connect your email in **Settings → Connected Accounts** if you want consolidated email \+ LinkedIn outreach threads in the CRM.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Stage 1 — Audit your LinkedIn sales readiness

**Goal:** Transform your LinkedIn profile from a job-seeking billboard into a sales showroom.

LinkedIn's algorithm prioritizes content for first-degree connections. Before you build your network or post thought leadership, your profile needs to convert the visitors that content brings. This stage audits where you stand and tells you exactly what to fix.

**Run the work plan:** Go to **New Chat → Select a workplan** and choose **Founder\_Linkedin\_Sales\_Readiness\_Audit**.

<Warning>
  A similar plan called `Founder_Linkedin_Sales_Readiness_Eval` also exists — it's a thinner, 2-step version. **Use the Audit**, which is the 7-step version with full scoring.
</Warning>

Emma asks for your name and LinkedIn URL, then reads your live profile (not a cached search-engine version — this requires LinkedIn Outreach to be connected). She scores you across 14 dimensions:

**Profile (40% of score):** headline, photo, banner, about section, featured section, experience, skills and endorsements, recommendations.

**Content (60% of score):** posting frequency, ICP relevance, content mix, engagement quality, hooks and formatting, CTA usage.

You get a tier — Sales-Ready (80–100), Almost There (60–79), Needs Work (40–59), or Foundation Missing (0–39) — with specific fixes for each gap.

**What founders most commonly miss:** the Featured section. LinkedIn gives you five slots to pin testimonials, demo links, case studies, or product launches. Most early-stage founders leave it empty or stale. Emma flags this and recommends what to put there.

**After the audit:** follow Emma's recommendations, then re-run the audit to confirm the score moved. Once you're in the 60\+ range, move to Stage 2.

## Stage 2 — Build your ICP network on LinkedIn

**Goal:** Fill your first-degree connections with people who match your ICP, so your content reaches them organically.

This is why Stage 1 matters: LinkedIn shows your posts primarily to first-degree connections. If your network is former colleagues and college friends, your thought leadership about B2B SaaS infrastructure never reaches the people who would buy it. Building ICP connections first means your content works from day one.

**How to run it:** Open a new chat in **Agent Mode** and describe who you want to connect with. Be specific:

_"Find founders at seed-stage B2B SaaS companies in the Bay Area who previously worked at Google, Meta, or Amazon and are currently in stealth or pre-launch."_

Emma uses the **LinkedIn Outreach** skill to search, identify matching leads, and draft personalized connection requests.

<Warning>
  **Emma drafts; you approve.** Connection requests are not sent automatically. Emma surfaces the leads and drafts the message, then waits for your confirmation before sending. Volume is capped (~40/day on LinkedIn Premium) and varies across days to stay within LinkedIn's terms of service.
</Warning>

**Then put the drafting on a schedule — not the sending.** Once you've run this interactively enough times to know the criteria and message style work, ask Emma to create a scheduled chat that prepares the batch for you:

_"Every weekday at 9am, find 10 new leads matching my ICP criteria, enrich and score them, draft connection requests, and email me the list for review. Do not send anything."_

The schedule does the finding, enriching, scoring, and drafting. **You still approve and send in a separate interactive session** — typically 15 minutes with your morning coffee. Every lead is parked in your **CRM** whether or not you send to them, so the list grows either way.

<Warning>
  **A scheduled run will not stop to ask you.** Unattended runs proceed best-effort rather than pausing for confirmation, so the approval gate above exists only in interactive chat. If a scheduled prompt is capable of sending, it will send. Write the plan so its last step is a draft, and re-read the prompt after every edit.

  This is the second rung of the [autonomy ladder](/playbooks/operating-rhythm#the-autonomy-ladder) — don't skip to it on day one. Run Stage 2 by hand until you can predict what Emma will produce, then schedule the drafting, and only later reduce how much of each batch you read.
</Warning>

**Why this matters for content:** every accepted connection is a person who will see your next LinkedIn post in their feed. The larger your ICP-dense network, the more effective your organic content becomes — without spending a dollar on ads.

## Stage 3 — Enrich, score, and manage your leads

**Goal:** Know who your leads actually are, score them against your ICP, and manage outreach from one place.

Leads arrive in your CRM from multiple paths: LinkedIn outreach (Stage 2), landing page signups, event registrations, and manual imports. This stage turns names into actionable profiles.

**Enrich your leads:** Open a lead list in **CRM** and ask Emma to enrich it:

_"Enrich the leads in my Workshop Attendees list."_

Emma runs a preview on 2 leads first, asks you to confirm, then enriches the rest. Enrichment pulls from multiple sources (including Apollo and LinkedIn API) server-side — you don't need API keys or separate tool subscriptions. Fields populated include company size, industry, funding stage, revenue range, tech stack, and LinkedIn profile data.

Every enriched value carries a provenance label — **grounded**, **web-unverified**, or **ai-knowledge** — so you know which facts are confirmed versus inferred.

**Score against your ICP:** Scoring is a separate step from enrichment. Ask Emma:

_"Score each lead in this list against my ICP."_

Emma reads your Market Strategy ICPs and scores each lead 0–100 with a reason. This tells you who to prioritize for outreach versus who to nurture with content.

**Manage outreach in one place:** Each lead record in the CRM consolidates all touchpoints — LinkedIn messages, email threads, and engagement history — in a single view. Emma can draft follow-up messages and suggest outreach sequences based on where each lead is in the conversation.

## Stage 4 — Prepare for every conversation

**Goal:** Walk into every meeting, event, or call knowing who you're talking to, what to discuss, and what to avoid.

When a lead accepts your connection request and you schedule a meeting, don't wing it. This stage gives you a research-backed prep playbook in minutes.

**Run the work plan:** Go to **New Chat → Select a workplan** and choose **Prospect Decode Playbook**.

<Note>
  The work plan is called **Prospect Decode Playbook**. There's also a _skill_ called "Decode Anyone Playbook" — same capability, different surface. Use the work plan from the picker for the full guided experience.
</Note>

Paste the person's LinkedIn URL. Emma researches their professional background — career history, recent activity, company news — using public data plus your CRM records and Fathom meeting transcripts (if connected).

The output is a branded HTML playbook containing:

- **Career summary** — who they are in 3–5 sentences.
- **DOs and DON'Ts** — specific to this person, with reasons and sources.
- **Insight cards** — 3–5 cards with confidence labels (Verified / Sourced / Inferred) and clickable source links.
- **Shared context** — real overlaps between you and them (not forced connections).
- **Conversation angles** — 2–3 openers with a one-line setup each.
- **Prior history** — CRM and Fathom records if they exist.

**For events:** the playbook supports batch mode. If you have a lead list of event attendees, Emma can decode the entire list and produce a playbook per person — or run it as a scheduled chat before the event.

_"I'm attending \[event name\] next Thursday. Decode every lead in my Event Attendees list and email me the playbooks by Wednesday."_

## What comes after your first 10 customers

Founder-led sales gets you from zero to your first paying customers. Once you've closed 10, the motion shifts — and how the motions compose from here is covered in [the pipeline system](/playbooks/pipeline-system). You'll likely add:

- **Organic content** — LinkedIn posts, X threads, newsletters, run as a sustained cadence. See [the content engine](/playbooks/content-engine).
- **Paid ads** — but only once your message is proven. Check the readiness gate in [paid ads on a founder budget](/playbooks/paid-ads) before spending.
- **Scaled outbound** — multi-touch email and LinkedIn sequences with the Cross-Channel Sequence Orchestrator.
- **A weekly rhythm** — so pipeline arrives predictably rather than in bursts. See [operating rhythm](/playbooks/operating-rhythm).

Emma handles all of these from the same workspace. Your brand guidelines, ICP personas, CRM, and Knowledge Base carry forward — nothing restarts. The playbook you built here becomes the foundation for everything that scales on top of it.
